In this work we add metagradients to the large-scale machine learning toolkit.
To do so, we overcome two challenges: (a) calculating metagradients at scale and
(b) modifying learning algorithms to be metasmooth---i.e., to admit
metagradients that locally predict model behavior. We then successfully
calculate and apply metagradients for large-scale models (up to 2B parameters)
to select data for CLIP pretraining and instruction fine-tuning, 
to (Huber) poison training data to decrease overall model accuracy, 
and search for high-dimensional hyperparameters (per-iteration learning rates). 
Given the successful applications of metagradients in these settings, we are
excited to see what unlocking metagradients enables in other areas of machine
learning.
